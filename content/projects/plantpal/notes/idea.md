# The idea

**Problem:** people forget to water plants, or water them too much. Fixed schedules ignore light,
season and pot size.

**Audience:** apartment owners with 5–20 plants, first-time plant parents.

**Bet:** a reminder app that adjusts the schedule from one question after each watering:
"Was the soil dry?"
