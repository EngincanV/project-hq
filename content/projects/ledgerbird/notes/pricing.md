# Pricing

| Plan | Price | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | 3 clients, Ledgerbird footer on invoices |
| Pro | $9 / month | Unlimited clients, recurring invoices, reminders |

Yearly: two months free.

## Why these numbers
- Interviewed freelancers pay $10–20 for similar tools and complain about features they never use.
- Free tier must be useful on its own: one client is not enough, three is.
